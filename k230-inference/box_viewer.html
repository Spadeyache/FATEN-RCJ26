      Connect to the Teensy's USB serial. The Teensy must be running yacheK230D
      printBoxes() output (format: K230D BOX cls=… score=… x1=… y1=… x2=… y2=…).
      Each K230D BOXES n=… line resets the canvas and the boxes that
      follow are drawn in sensor-frame coords (640×480). What you see here should
      mirror what's drawn on the K230D's IDE preview — if they differ, the wire
      payload is being parsed wrong on one side.
