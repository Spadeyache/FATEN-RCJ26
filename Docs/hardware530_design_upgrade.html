Hardware Design Upgrade
RCJ RescueLine / 2026 Build Plan

After the USA international competition we mapped every place the robot lost time or failed, and turned each into one concrete design change. The course was longer, bumpier and steeper than our regional runs — the line section alone took ~4 min. Five upgrades below; the proven parts of the current robot are kept untouched.

Drive & Chassis
01 · OFF-ROAD
Off-road capability
Tire mold · same size, new tread

Why At the USA comp the robot struggled on bumps and uneven terrain — it lost grip and got hung up where the surface was not flat.

Plan Keep the tire size (geometry and gearing stay valid), but change the mold material and tread shape for more compliance and bite over bumps. New tires are cast from a new mold — see the Parts plan.

02 · SPEED
Robot speed
In-wheel STS servo · planned ~×1.5
target×1.5

Why The international course is longer and more complex. The USA line section took ~4 min; with the bigger, harder international terrain we need margin to finish in time.

Plan Move the STS servo into the tire (in-wheel drive) and step the ratio up by 40/26 ≈ ×1.5. This is the planned target — a ~1.5× top speed should comfortably absorb the longer course.

03 · ROTATIONAL AXIS
Adjustable rotational axis
Per-wheel motor power · pivot control

Why On a slope the nose lifts (noses up): gravity drags the lighter end downhill and the robot rotates off the line. We want to move the rotation axis to fight this — by changing how much power each wheel gets, not by adding any new mechanism.

Tried On the bench we drove the four wheels independently and watched where it pivots: front 100 / back 0 → it moves slowly with the axis toward the back; the opposite gain (front 0 / back 100) moves fast with the axis toward the front. So the pivot really does shift with power balance — but it is dominated by which tires grip, so it depends heavily on front/rear grip balance (the new tire mold feeds directly into this).

Bench finding — the pivot follows the power balance
Mission Systems
04 · TOF UNIT
ToF unit — evacuation
Distance sensing · exit detection

Why In the evacuation zone the robot must find and confirm the exit. Vision alone is unreliable for the walls/opening geometry.

Plan Add a ToF unit to measure wall distances and determine the exit of the evacuation zone.

05 · ARM
Arm with dropper
Grab + release · victim ball

Why The robot needs to collect the victim ball and place it in the correct zone.

Plan Build an arm with a dropper mechanism — one motion to grab the ball, one to drop it on release.
