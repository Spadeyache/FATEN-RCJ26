Parts & Fabrication
RCJ RescueLine / Off-design tasks

Outside the design work, two fabrication tasks gate the build: validating the new PCB and casting the new tires from a fresh mold. Kept simple on purpose — these are make-and-check items, not design decisions.

Production tasks
01 · PCB
PCB test
Bench-verify the new board

Why The upgrades (in-wheel servo wiring, ToF, arm) ride on a revised board — it must be proven on the bench before assembly.

Do Check power rails, the servo bus and every sensor header (ToF, IMU, camera), then confirm a clean motor + sensor bring-up.

02 · TIRE MOLD
Tire mold creation
Same size · new material & shape

Why New tires drive two upgrades at once: off-road capability (more compliance and bite over bumps) and rotational-axis adjustment (the pivot is set by front/rear grip balance, so the tire material/tread is what makes the axis controllable).

Do Make a new mold — keep the size, change the material and tread shape — and cast the tires.
